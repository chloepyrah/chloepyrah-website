Workload and SaaS health

With most of the team working remotely, leaders needed one place to see both the work and the tools our clients relied on. This view brought the two together.

The weekly status chart showed how much work was complete, in progress, blocked or not started, so team leaders could rebalance workload before deadlines slipped. The hub status line tracked every customer-facing Insight Hub over 30 days, so a blocked hub was spotted and fixed before a client noticed.
