The guides

Europe 10

‹ Italy · Sep 2026

Sicily

  • Stay: Book a farmhouse over a hotel
  • Eat: Anything from the menu boards at Ristorante a Scicli
  • Do: Book a classic yacht day trip from Syracuse
  • Tip: Go to Modica and explore its streets of wine bars
‹ Italy · Jul 2025

Sorrento, Amalfi & Ravello

  • Stay: Book a guest house as high up as you can go for the most beautiful views
  • Eat: Neapolitan pizza wherever you can
  • Do: A day trip to Capri by boat
  • Tip: Invest in a private transfer from Naples airport; public transport is busy and difficult
‹ Italy · Jul 2025

Capri

  • Stay: Stay in Anacapri to avoid the crowds of the main areas
  • Eat: Le Camerelle
  • Do: An evening shopping spree followed by al fresco Aperols
  • Tip: Don't rely on Google Maps, and try to stay close to a main road. It's easy to get lost!
  • Cocktails: La Zagara
‹ Italy · Aug 2018

Lake Garda

  • Stay: Stay in Riva del Garda to avoid the crowds
  • Eat: Local fish dishes, freshly caught from the lake
  • Do: Catamaran hire is essential!
  • Tip: It's a cycling hub, so hire some bikes in an area set up for exploring that way
‹ Spain · Jun 2024

Barcelona

  • Stay: The Hoxton, Poblenou
  • Eat: Local cured meats with local cheeses
  • Do: Time your trip for a music festival; Barcelona hosts some of the best in the world
  • Tip: A sunset trip to Montjuïc Castle for the best views of the city skyline
‹ Netherlands

Rotterdam

  • Eat: Sandwiches at Guliano
  • Do: An afternoon at Depot Boijmans Van Beuningen
  • Tip: Time your visit for King's Day to see the city at its best
‹ UK

Manchester

  • Stay: The top-floor suites at Native
  • Eat: Okra fries at Bundobust
  • Do: An afternoon shopping in the Northern Quarter and an evening drinking in Ancoats
  • Tip: Look out for the symbol of Manchester everywhere: the worker bee!
  • Coffee: Federal
  • Cocktails: Tomato margarita at Stray
‹ UK

London

  • Stay: The Standard, King's Cross
  • Eat: Bacon & egg naan breakfast at Dishoom
  • Do: Lime bike to Victoria Park and grab a takeaway pizza and some tinnies
  • Coffee: Allpress Dalston
  • Cocktails: SOMA
‹ UK

Cotswolds

  • Stay: The Bull in Burford
  • Eat: The slow-cooked pork at Worton Kitchen Garden
  • Do: A day trip to the grounds of Blenheim Palace
  • Cocktails: Soho Farmhouse
‹ UK

Sheffield

  • Stay: Book a hotel on the outskirts, closer to the Peak District
  • Eat: Small plates from Bench
  • Do: A day trip to the Peak District
  • Tip: Visit on Tramlines weekend to see the city at its best
  • Coffee: Anything along Sharrow Vale
  • Cocktails: The Pearl on Park Hill

Asia 13

‹ Malaysia · Oct 2026

Kuala Lumpur

  • Stay: The Chow Kit
  • Eat: Take a drive out to River View Seafood Restaurant in Kuala Selangor
  • Do: Book shopping at REXKL
  • Tip: Do the National Museum of Malaysia on your first day to learn about the country
  • Cocktails: The Listening Bar
‹ Malaysia · Apr 2026

Penang

  • Stay: The Blue Mansion
  • Eat: Get up early for a Toh Soon Cafe breakfast. Soft eggs with charcoal toast.
  • Do: Boutique shopping at Six Senses and 2mm
  • Tip: Send a postcard from The Postcard Shop
  • Coffee: Heim
  • Cocktails: Curios-City
‹ Malaysia · Mar 2026

Langkawi

  • Stay: Bon Ton Kampung Houses
  • Eat: Beach BBQ satay spots
  • Do: A morning trip around the north-east mangroves, then an afternoon dip at Seven Wells Waterfall
  • Tip: Rent a car from the beach high streets for a cheaper price and drive everywhere
  • Coffee: Ngopi Pantai
‹ Malaysia · Mar 2023

Sandakan

  • Stay: The Shangri-La Rasa Ria
  • Eat: A local-style laksa
  • Do: Sail away from the shore for an incredible view of Mount Kinabalu
  • Tip: Book a tour guide for the orangutans; they will get you to the best viewing spots (and tip your guide)
‹ Thailand · Oct 2026

Bangkok

  • Stay: The Mustang Blu
  • Eat: Khao soi may be a northern Thai dish, but it will always be my number one!
  • Do: An evening hopping between XXG, Wallflower and Tep Bar
  • Tip: An afternoon in Song Wat and Talat Noi
  • Coffee: Piccolo Vicolo Cafe, Ratchathewi
  • Cocktails: Dry Wave
‹ Thailand · Oct 2026

Pattaya

  • Stay: The Standard Hotel
  • Eat: Walk along the beach and find a local BBQ seafood joint
  • Do: A catamaran day trip out to sea
  • Tip: Stay and play in Jomtien to escape the chaos of central Pattaya
‹ Thailand · Jul 2026

Khao Yai

  • Stay: Sala Villas
  • Eat: Sunset BBQ at Midwinter
  • Do: Morning safari followed by wine tasting at PB Valley
  • Tip: Book safaris ahead of time; guides can get fully booked
‹ Cambodia · Sep 2026

Siem Reap

  • Stay: Mango Kesararam
  • Do: Make sure to visit the Landmine Museum as well as the main attractions
  • Tip: Bring US dollars in cash; they will get you further than local cash or card payments
‹ Japan · Apr 2026

Tokyo

  • Stay: Around Yoyogi Park, for a nice view but close to Harajuku and Shibuya
  • Eat: Less sushi, more ramen
  • Do: Vintage shopping at Amore, Hive and Casanova
  • Tip: Drinking in Golden Gai is essential
‹ Japan · Apr 2019

Kyoto

  • Stay: Guest houses over hotels! Great hosts and hospitality at HoNoDo COBO
  • Eat: Beef noodle bowls after a cold walk
  • Do: Walk to the top of the hill at the deer park for an amazing view
  • Tip: Go as early as possible for the bamboo groves
‹ Japan · Apr 2019

Hakuba

  • Stay: Morino Ski Lodge
  • Eat: A bowl of ramen on top of a mountain
  • Do: Make time for a post-ski onsen dip
  • Tip: Ski a couple of days out of season for quiet slopes
‹ Sri Lanka · Feb 2026

Hiriketiya

  • Stay: MOND
  • Eat: Smoke & Bitters
  • Do: An early-morning surfing lesson
  • Tip: Go out dancing at least once!
  • Coffee: Bunch
  • Cocktails: RAA
‹ Sri Lanka · Feb 2026

Yala

  • Stay: DoubleTree by Hilton
  • Do: The earliest possible safari, to see the sunrise over the national park
  • Tip: Group trips are just as good as the private ones

Rest of the world 2

‹ UAE · Nov 2023

Dubai

  • Eat: Daily portions of hummus
  • Do: Take a yacht or catamaran trip to see the skyline from the water
  • Tip: Sunset in the sand dunes is a must!
‹ Morocco · Jun 2025

Marrakesh

  • Eat: A lamb and prune tagine once nearly brought me to tears
  • Do: Visit the Amal Women's Training Center for a cooking class
  • Tip: Shop for gorgeous homewares and leather shoes in the souks
